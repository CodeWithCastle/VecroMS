01 · Brand

Green means go. Yellow means look.

The palette is sampled from the existing brand mark: a green car outline with yellow windows. VecroMS keeps the symbol, replaces the wordmark, and uses the green for action and the yellow for attention.

V
VecroMSFleet operations, clearly.

The mark is a rounded green tile with a white V and a yellow signal dot. Clearspace is half the tile height. Minimum width is 96px.

#00923FBrand green
#FFF500Brand yellow

Voice

  • Direct. Verb + object: “Create job”, “Assign technician”.
  • Specific. Name the record: “Delete booking BK-1042?”
  • Calm. Errors say what happened and how to fix it.
  • Not cute. No “Oops”, no exclamation marks, no blame.

Rules

  • Sentence case everywhere except the overline label.
  • Max three type sizes inside one card.
  • Tabular numbers for money, counts and metrics.
  • Line length 60–75 characters; prose max 680px.
  • No uppercase data, no centred paragraphs, no text below 12px.

08 · Accessibility

AA is the floor.

Every colour pair below was measured. Every component spec includes keyboard, focus and screen-reader behaviour. Test with a keyboard, not just a mouse.

Checklist

  • Focus is visible on every interactive element: 2px ring, 2px offset.
  • Labels are visible; placeholders are examples, never labels.
  • Status is a dot and a label — never colour alone.
  • Tables have a caption, scoped headers and aria-sort.
  • Dialogs trap focus, close on Esc and return focus to the trigger.
  • Toasts use aria-live; errors are assertive.
  • Targets are ≥ 24px; 44px on touch.
  • Reduced motion, 200% zoom and 320px reflow are tested.
